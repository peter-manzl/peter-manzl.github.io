Robotics · Multibody Dynamics · Machine Learning

Dr. techn. Peter Manzl

Group leader Robotics at the University of Augsburg. I work on robot-based testing and manufacturing, simulation of multibody systems, and AI methods that make simulation and control faster and more reliable.

Research Publications CV University profile

What I work on

Robot-based testing & manufacturing

Force-controlled and multi-robot systems for inspection, component testing, and bending of large and complex parts.

Multibody dynamics & simulation

Flexible multibody models, contact and friction, and the open-source simulation code Exudyn with a focus on robotics, including Mecanum wheels, flexible links and compliant joints.

Machine learning and Artificial Intelligence (AI) for dynamics

Surrogate models such as SLIDE, reinforcement learning for mechanical systems, and language models that generate simulation models.

Mobile manipulation

Base placement and design of mobile manipulators, including the omnidirectional LeoBot built for the RoboCup@Work league.
